# A visit ends when the darts are taken out, not at the third dart

A player's visit closes on the board's takeout signal, not when the game rules say it is over (third dart, bust or checkout). That matches how the room plays: the next player only steps up once the board is clear. After a third dart, bust or checkout the match waits for takeout, and further darts before it are ignored. A manual "next player" control stands in when a takeout isn't detected.
