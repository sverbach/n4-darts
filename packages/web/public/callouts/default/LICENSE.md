# Default caller voice

Generated by `tools/callouts/generate.sh`.

- Voice: Piper `en_GB-northern_english_male-medium` from [rhasspy/piper-voices](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices) (MIT). It was fine-tuned on the Northern English male speaker of [OpenSLR 83](https://www.openslr.org/83/), *Crowdsourced high-quality UK and Ireland English Dialect speech data set* by Google (CC BY-SA 4.0).
- Its base model is Piper's `en_US-lessac-medium`, trained on the [Lessac Blizzard 2013](https://www.cstr.ed.ac.uk/projects/blizzard/2013/lessac_blizzard2013/license.html) recordings, which are licensed for research only. These recordings may carry that restriction, so they're meant for running n4 on your own server, not for reuse or redistribution elsewhere.
- Generator: piper-tts 1.8.0 (GPL-3.0-or-later). It's used only to make the files and isn't shipped; the recordings it outputs aren't covered by its licence.
- These recordings are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, as the OpenSLR 83 data asks, subject to the base model's restriction above. Attribution: Google, OpenSLR 83; voice model by the Piper project.
- `tools/callouts/generate.sh ljspeech` makes a public-domain alternative (US female voice trained only on LJ Speech).
